Our story

One room, one laser, and a lot of sawdust.

Sawdust & Co. started in a two-car garage with a secondhand laser cutter and a stack of scrap oak. What began as gifts for family turned into a small studio making coasters, wall art, and custom commissions — still cut, sanded, and packed by hand, one order at a time.

We keep the catalog small on purpose. Every design goes through dozens of test cuts before it ships, and we'd rather make two things well than ten things adequately.

Everything is cut from FSC-certified hardwood sourced from domestic mills, finished with food-safe oil, and shipped in plastic-free packaging.
